Accessibility information

  • We’re expecting high footfall at this event; please expect crowds, with peak hours from 11am–2pm. Please consider this when planning to attend.
  • Ear plugs: collect from staff throughout the event (staff will be wearing cyan blue t-shirts).
  • Physical access: the Curzon Building is wheelchair accessible, but will be busy on the day – please let us know if you’d like support getting around the event.
  • Quiet Room: a designated room is located in C001. Access it through the Library – enter the library, turn immediately right and go towards the end of the room to find C001. If you need a silent room to relax, please visit our Calm Space (C144) on the first floor.

Walking:
The City Centre Campus is a short 10-15 minute walk from Birmingham Moor Street Station, and a 20-25 minute walk from Birmingham New Street Station.

Bus:
Most bus services terminate in the city centre. Moor Street Queensway and the Priory Queensway Interchanges are the closest to City Centre Campus. The 14 bus route stops on Cardigan Street (near campus).

If it is your first time using a bus in Birmingham, we recommend paying with a contactless method – just tap your card on the reader every time you board a bus and you won’t be charged more than the maximum DaySaver rate, no matter how many times you travel.
